# Artwork in the store screenshots

The screenshots show real films whose posters may be shown freely: films made by the Blender Foundation under Creative
Commons Attribution licences, and public-domain classics. The posters are shrunk copies from Wikimedia Commons. Channel logos
and the rest of the demo (channel names, guide) are made up.
